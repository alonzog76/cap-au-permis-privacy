# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 14, 2026**

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application Cap au permis (Côtier) ("Application") for mobile devices, which was created by Antonio Argento.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Our Application does not collect, store, or share any personal data from its users. 

### Personal Information
We do not require you to provide any personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address) to download or use the Application.

### Non-Personal and Automatically Collected Data
* **Device Information:** The Application does not track your unique device ID, IP address, or hardware specifications.
* **Location Data:** The Application does not access, collect, or monitor your precise or approximate geographical location.

## 2. Third-Party Services
We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or data-tracking SDKs within the Application. Your data remains entirely private on your device.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
Since we do not collect any user data, we do not store, retain, or process any personal information on external servers.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our Application complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because we do not collect any personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
